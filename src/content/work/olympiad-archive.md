Competition problems are scattered across PDFs, forum threads, and national
websites in a dozen languages. The goal of this project is to combine them into one corpus, and tag them
by topic and difficulty using AI models. This will make it easier for both students and teachers to find problems related to what they're actually trying to learn/teach.
